# Art Credits

Owner: `graphics-designer`.

## M1: no third-party art
All M1 visuals are **original and procedural**. They are generated at runtime in TypeScript from Three.js primitives
in `src/render/procedural/` (characters, props, weapons, loot, toon materials, outlines). No external model, texture,
font, or icon files are used. The palette and designs are original to this project. See `docs/art/STYLE.md`.

## M2a: no third-party art
The M2a additions are also **original and procedural**: the dmr, sniper and lmg, all sights (iron, red dot, 4x and
8x scopes), the helmets and vests (tiers 1–3), the pineapple grenade, the sniper ammo box and the stance pivot are
built at runtime from Three.js primitives in `src/render/procedural/` (`weapons.ts`, `wearables.ts`,
`grenade.ts`, `loot.ts`, `chibi.ts`). The ADS reticles, scope frame and item icons in `docs/art/STYLE.md` §17–18
are original SVG drawn for this project. Gun shapes are generic toy blocks and do not copy any real-world model
or another game's design.

## M2b office: no third-party art
The office prop kit (`src/render/procedural/office.ts`) is original and procedural. The user's reference photo and
seating plan were used only for layout and general look (light wood floor, white desks, glass rooms, hanging
fluorescent panels). Nothing was traced or copied: no people's likeness, no names, no company name or logo, and
not the cartoon picture on the plan. The plush toys (seal, crab, whale) are original designs, unlike any plush in
the photo. The reference images stay in the repo root and are not shipped.

## M2b plane drop: no third-party art
The seaplane, the delivery drone and the parachute (`src/render/procedural/carrier.ts`) are original procedural toy
designs. They do not copy any real aircraft, drone product, brand livery or another game's drop ship.

## M2b first-person viewmodel: no third-party art
The chibi forearms, mitten hands and the hold / ADS poses (`src/render/procedural/viewmodel.ts`) are original and
procedural.

Three.js (MIT licence) is a code dependency, not an art asset. It is listed here only for completeness.
