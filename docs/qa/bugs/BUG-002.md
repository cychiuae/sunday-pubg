# BUG-002: Taking pointer lock applies a huge mouse delta, so the view snaps ~80° sideways and 35° up

- **Severity:** S3 (major). It happens on every lock, including Resume after Esc.
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18; `npx tsc` clean, `npx vitest run` 693/693.
- **Suspected owner:** game-developer (`src/game/input/InputController.ts`).

## Steps to reproduce
1. Join a match. When "Click to play" shows, click the button.
2. Watch the view, or log `MouseEvent.movementX/Y` while `document.pointerLockElement` is set.

## Expected
Taking the lock does not move the view. The player looks where they were looking (pitch 0 after spawn).

## Actual
The first `mousemove` delivered after the lock engages carries the cursor's jump back to the lock origin, and
`InputController` feeds it straight into `InputState.look()`. Measured in headless Chromium (Playwright 1.65), with the
probe logging locked mousemoves and the `pitch` of outgoing inputs:

```
1280x720: pitch before=0 after=0.605   locked mousemoves=["-639,-275,true","0,0,true",...]
 960x540:                              locked mousemoves=["-480,-270,true","0,0,true",...]
```

At the default sensitivity (0.0022 rad/px) that is a yaw jump of about 1.4 rad and pitch +0.605 rad (35° up). With the
pitch up, the over-the-shoulder camera pulls in behind the head (see BUG-003), so the first thing a player sees after
"Click to play" can be the back of their own head.

Chromium is known to deliver such a re-centring spike on the first locked move, and spikes also show up on some
Windows/Chrome setups. Whether real desktop Chrome/Firefox/Safari do it here needs a manual check, but the client
should be robust either way.

## Suggested fix
Ignore the first `mousemove` after `pointerlockchange` (locked), and/or drop single events with |movement| above a sane
cap (for example 300 px).

## Evidence
- Probe output above (spec `zz-probe`, removed after the run). The gameplay spec works around it by measuring yaw
  deltas only after the lock.
- Screenshots: `test-results/shots/gameplay-prompt.png` (camera under the umbrella canopy, looking up after lock),
  `test-results/shots/gameplay-outside-tide.png`.
