# BUG-003: Looking up, the camera pulls in behind the head and the local chibi fills the screen

- **Severity:** S3 (major). You can't see or aim at anything above about 35° (players on the summit, on rocks, or on
  a jump).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18.
- **Suspected owner:** graphics-engineer (`src/render/cameraRig.ts` / Renderer camera placement). Possibly the
  game-developer for the pivot and pitch range in `src/game/camera.ts` / tuning.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Join, land on open beach, and take pointer lock.
2. Move the mouse up (look up) step by step.

## Expected
The crosshair target stays visible at every allowed pitch (±80°). If the camera has to come close to the character,
the local model fades or hides (B9: "camera does not clip into terrain", and it should not sit inside the character).

## Actual
Camera height above the feet, read from `window.__render.camera` and `window.__game.localPos` on flat sand:

| Look | camera y − feet y |
|---|---|
| level-ish | 3.33 m |
| +35° | 1.64 m |
| +60° | 0.37 m |
| max (+80°) | 0.49 m |

From about +45° up, the camera sits at neck height right behind the head. The head and the ink outline hull cover most of
the screen, including the crosshair area.
