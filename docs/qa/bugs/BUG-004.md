# BUG-004: Minimap POI labels spill past and get clipped at the minimap edge

- **Severity:** S4 (cosmetic).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18.
- **Suspected owner:** ui-ux-designer (`src/ui/minimap.ts`).

## Steps to reproduce
1. Join a match at 1280×720 with UI size 100 %.
2. Look at the minimap in the top right.

## Expected
POI names are fully readable inside the minimap circle (UI.md checklist: "Minimap: coastline, POI names, ...").

## Actual
Labels near the rim are cut at the canvas edge or overlap the frame: "Sunny Seconds Wreck" shows as "Sunn" + "Seconds
Wreck", "Hammock Hamlet" as "ammock Hamlet", and "Rickety Dock" runs past the right edge. Labels in the centre also
overlap the zone ring and the self arrow ("Sunburn Summit" / "Grumpy Rocks"). At 960×540 the labels are not drawn at all
(that may be intended).

## Suggested fix
Anchor labels inward (right-aligned on the east side, left-aligned on the west), clamp them to the circle, or use
shorter minimap names (narrative-designer) for the rim POIs.
