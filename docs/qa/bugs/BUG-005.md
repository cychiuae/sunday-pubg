# BUG-005: The headroom rule can never fire on the island, so "cannot stand under the hut roof" is not reachable

- **Severity:** minor (spec/acceptance gap; no player-facing malfunction).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18 (M2a Phase C complete, no git).
- **Suspected owner:** level-designer (hut collider / map) with game-designer (stance.md headroom case). game-developer only if a fix needs sim work.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `npx vitest run tests/sim/m2a-edge.test.ts -t "hut"` and `-t "raised palm"`.
2. Read `src/shared/sim/colliders.ts` `PROP_SHAPES.hut`: four ground-rooted boxes (`bottom = -Infinity`), the widest
   `hx = hz = 2.05`.

## Expected
2a-C1 acceptance: "cannot stand under the hut roof". The brief names the hut roof as the main headroom case, so some
place on the island should block C/Z/Space -> taller stance with `noHeadroom` ("No room to stand up").

## Actual
- The hut collider is a solid prism from the ground up (closed door, no interior), so no player can ever be under its
  roof. A prone player pushed against each wall stands up normally (test passes: no false `noHeadroom`).
- The only floating colliders on the island are the upper palm-trunk segments; the lowest one is more than
  `stand.height + headroomMargin` (1.35 m) above the ground beneath it (test asserts this).
- So `stance.headroomCheck` is dead in play. The rule itself works on a synthetic raised slab (stance.md AT7, owner test
  `stance.test.ts` "AT7: headroom under a raised collider").

## Suggested fix
Either accept it (reword the 2a-C1 criterion to "the rule works under a raised collider" and drop the hut wording), or
add real overhead cover to the map: for example a raised hut floor on stilts with a crawl space, or a low lean-to whose
roof collider bottom is 0.55-1.3 m above the ground, so crouching or crawling under it and pressing Space shows
"No room to stand up".

## Evidence
`tests/sim/m2a-edge.test.ts`: "hut: a prone player hugging each wall of a hut can stand up ..." and "raised palm trunk
segments (the only floating colliders on the island) are far above a standing player".

## Status: closed (not applicable), 2026-11-18, M2b Phase D
Decision recorded in `docs/design/systems/stance.md` ("Headroom on the shipped maps (BUG-005 decision): N/A on both
maps in M2b; the rule stays") and AT7/AT7b. The headroom rule itself is covered by the synthetic-slab test
(`src/shared/sim/stance.test.ts` AT7); `src/game/levels/office.test.ts` "lists every raised part whose bottom sits
0.6-1.35 m above a walkable top as a deliberate gap" guards the office. No product change expected.
