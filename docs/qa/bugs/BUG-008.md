# BUG-008: Crouched and prone chibis stick out above their hitboxes (the visible head top cannot be hit)

- **Severity:** major (players aim at what they see; head shots on crouched/prone targets miss with no feedback).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18.
- **Suspected owner:** game-designer (`movement.stances.*.height`, `combat.hitbox` in balance.json) together with
  graphics-designer (`CHIBI_STANCES` in `src/render/procedural/chibi.ts`). One of the two must move to meet the other.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `npx vitest run tests/render --silent=false`
2. Read the `[prone-hitbox]` and `[crouch-hitbox]` lines (the tests are `it.fails` and stay green until this is fixed).

## Expected
ADR 0004 §2 / STYLE.md §16: the posed chibi lies inside the sim hitbox for its stance (the standing chibi is 1.39 m
against a 1.30 m cylinder, so a ~0.1 m overhang is the established tolerance).

## Actual
Measured on `createChibi` at scale 1 (render uses scale 1) with the shipped `CHIBI_STANCES` poses:
- **Prone:** sim box is 0.5 m tall (`stances.prone.height`). The head mesh spans y 0.18-0.86 m, so its top 0.36 m is
  above the box. A horizontal shot at the visible head at y = 0.6 misses (stance.md AT11 even asserts this miss).
  Torso (0.39 m), legs (0.28 m) and the box footprint (x +-0.4, z +-0.75) are fine. The head mesh centre is at
  z = -0.23, just behind the head zone's back edge (-0.25), so the rear half of the visible head counts as body.
- **Crouch:** sim cylinder is 0.95 m (`stances.crouch.height`). The crouched head top is at about 1.24 m with the
  shipped crouch drop (0.16 m) and lean; the art spec comment itself says "~1.13 m". Either way 0.18-0.29 m of the
  visible head is above the cylinder, and stance.md AT10 asserts a shot at y = 1.0 misses a crouched target.

## Suggested fix
Pick one source of truth:
- raise `stances.crouch.height` to about 1.15 and `stances.prone.height` to about 0.8 (and re-run the stance/
  shots-to-kill tests and AT10/AT11 numbers), or
- lower the poses (bigger crouch drop, head kept low when prone, e.g. smaller `headUp`) until the head fits.

Note: ADR 0004 §2 makes collision height = hitbox height, so raising the heights also changes headroom and what fits
under M2b desks; lowering the poses has no gameplay side effects.

## Evidence
`tests/render/prone-hitbox-match.test.ts` output:
`sim box x +-0.4 z +-0.75 y 0..0.5; visual head x -0.27..0.26 y 0.18..0.86 z -0.55..0.09` and
`crouched head top 1.24 m vs crouch hitbox height 0.95 m`; standing chibi 0.00..1.39 m.

## Status: closed (fixed), 2026-11-18, M2b Phase D
Verified: prone height is 0.6 m (stance.md "Visual fit"), and the two `it.fails` in
`tests/render/prone-hitbox-match.test.ts` are plain `it` now and pass with precise bounds
(`Box3.setFromObject(mesh, true)`): torso, head and legs lie inside the prone box, and the crouched head top is under
the crouch height (+ 0.12 m tolerance). The graphics side is also covered by `src/render/m2b.test.ts` "BUG-008 head
fit" (prone at pitch -30 / 0 / +45, crouch tilt).
