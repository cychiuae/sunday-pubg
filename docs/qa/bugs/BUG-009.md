# BUG-009: "Helmet" label overflows its HUD gear cell at 960x540

- **Severity:** minor (text overflow, cosmetic).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18.
- **Suspected owner:** ui-ux-designer (`src/ui/hud.ts` gear row, `src/ui/ui.css` `.ui-gear`).

## Steps to reproduce
1. Window 960x540, UI size 100 %.
2. Join a match and look at the vitals panel (bottom left): the vest and helmet cells next to the stance icon.

## Expected
Labels fit inside their cells (UX check "text overflow"), as "Vest" does.

## Actual
"Helmet" runs into the right border of its cell; the final "t" overlaps the frame. Same in every M2a E2E screenshot
at this size.

## Suggested fix
Let the gear cells size to content, shrink the label, or use the icon plus tier pips without the word at small sizes.

## Evidence
`test-results/shots/m2a-prone.png`, `test-results/shots/m2a-cooking.png`, `test-results/shots/m2a-scoped.png`
(bottom left), from `tests/e2e/m2a.spec.ts`.

## Status: closed (fixed), 2026-11-18, M2b Phase D
Verified in `tests/e2e/m2a.spec.ts` "HUD: vest and helmet cells ..." at 960x540: for every `.ui-gear` cell, no
descendant extends past the cell's right edge and `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` (see the spec notes line
"gear cells at 960x540"); screenshot `test-results/shots/m2a-hud-gear-960.png`.
