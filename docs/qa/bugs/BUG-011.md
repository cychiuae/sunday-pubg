# BUG-011: A sprint-jump from either office perch touches down on a monitor top (plateau gap rule broken)

- **Severity:** minor (nobody can stay on the monitor: the touchdown lasts 1-2 ticks and the player slides off onto
  the desk below with no damage. But a jump pressed on that tick chains from 11.03 m to a 12.08 m apex, which gives
  the perch an extra hop that the level design does not intend. It also fails maps.md acceptance 17: "every plateau gap
  is <= 3 m or >= 6 m").
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18 (after the M2b C-gate, PROTOCOL_VERSION 3).
- **Suspected owner:** level-designer (`src/game/levels/office.ts`, perch placement) and graphics-designer
  (`src/shared/kits/office.ts` `monitorColliders`, screen part). If the fix is "screen tops are not walkable", the
  game-developer needs a non-walkable collider flag in the sim (colliders are flat-topped prisms today).

## Steps to reproduce
1. `npx vitest run tests/sim/m2b-edge.test.ts -t "monitor"`.
2. The `it.fails` test "BUG-011 (fails until fixed): no sprint-jump from either perch ends standing on a monitor top"
   sprint-jumps from the end cell of PERCH-NE and PERCH-SW (5 x 3 start points, 24 headings, 8 jump timings each) on
   the real OFFICE map with the office balance.
3. Minimal manual repro: stand on the NE perch at (78.0, 10.44, -27.5), face +Z (yaw pi), sprint and jump about 0.3 m
   before the perch's north edge.

## Expected
From a perch (top 10.44 m) no sprint-jump lands on any walkable top other than the perch itself or the levels below
(maps.md 2.4: gaps are <= 3 m or >= 6 m, no near-misses). Monitors are canopies, not cover and not platforms.

## Actual
- TR S k4 monitor (prop 87, centre (77.36, -21.315), screen box 4.95 x 0.36 m, top 11.025 m) is 0.585 m above the NE
  perch top and 3.76 m from its north edge (z -25.255 to -21.495). A sprint-jump covers up to 4.7 m with apex 1.056 m,
  so it reaches the screen top.
- 23 of 2880 sprint-jumps from PERCH-NE and 23 of 2880 from PERCH-SW end grounded on a monitor top at y 11.025, e.g.
  `from (78.92, -26.61) yaw 180 jump@4 -> monitor prop 87 at (78.92, 11.025, -21.49)` and
  `from (-64.72, 19.82) yaw 194 jump@6 -> monitor ... at (-63.33, 11.025, 24.99)`.
- The touchdown lasts 1-2 ticks at ~7.2 m/s; 0 of 150 landings could stay on the top, even holding S right after
  touchdown. A jump on the touchdown tick works: apex 12.08 m, landing around (77.5, 5.27, -13.0).

## Suggested fix (pick one)
- Move the perches so the nearest monitor top is >= 6 m away or below the perch top (level-designer).
- Make monitor screen tops non-walkable (thin slanted top, or a sim flag so the screen blocks but never supports;
  graphics-designer + game-developer).

## Evidence
- `tests/sim/m2b-edge.test.ts`: "geometry: TR S k4 monitor top sits 0.585 m above the NE perch top ...",
  "BUG-011 (fails until fixed) ..." (it.fails), "BUG-011 detail: the touchdown on the monitor top lasts 1-2 ticks ...".
- Log line: `[monitor-top] PERCH-NE: 23 of 2880 sprint-jumps end on a monitor top`,
  `[monitor-top] touches 4, stays 0, chained apex 12.08 m`.

## Status: closed (fixed), 2026-11-18, M2c Phase 3
Verified on the M2c tree: thin raised kit boxes (min half width < player.radius / 2) are `noStand` in
`src/shared/sim/colliders.ts` (they block but never support), so single-monitor screens are no longer platforms.
`tests/sim/m2b-edge.test.ts` "BUG-011 (fixed): no sprint-jump from either perch ends standing on a monitor top" is a
plain `it` and passes: `[monitor-top] PERCH-NE: 0 of 2880`, `PERCH-SW: 0 of 2880`. The rewritten detail test shows 0
touchdowns and a max height of 11.50 m after leaving the perch (the perch jump itself; no chained jump). Owner test:
`src/shared/sim/bugs.m2c.test.ts` "BUG-011: monitor screens are canopies, not platforms".
Residual note (not a bug): the 6 dual-monitor screens (1.17 m deep) stay standable at 11.025 m, but nothing reaches
them (4.37 m above the desk top; 0 landings from either perch).
