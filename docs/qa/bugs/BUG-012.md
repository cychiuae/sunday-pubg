# BUG-012: The prone pitch clamp applies one tick after the prone transition starts

- **Severity:** minor (for one tick, 33 ms, a player who has started going prone keeps an out-of-range pitch, e.g.
  +70 deg, in the World and in the snapshot other players render. Firing and throwing are blocked during transitions,
  so there is no gameplay effect; a remote prone pose can flash at the wrong angle for one snapshot).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18 (after the M2b C-gate, PROTOCOL_VERSION 3).
- **Suspected owner:** game-developer (`World` input sanitize: `pitchLimits(move)` is evaluated with the stance
  before this tick's `stanceToggle` is applied; `src/shared/sim/World.ts` / `src/shared/sim/stance.ts`).

## Steps to reproduce
1. `npx vitest run tests/sim/m2b-edge.test.ts -t "BUG-012"` (an `it.fails` test).
2. Or: a standing player holds pitch +70 deg for a few ticks, then sends one input with `pitch: +70 deg,
   stanceToggle: 'prone'`.

## Expected
camera.md "Prone pitch": "Entering prone with pitch outside the range: the sim clamps on the first tick of the
transition"; acceptance 9: "entering prone at pitch +70 ends at +45 by the first prone tick".

## Actual
| Tick | stanceFrom -> stance | stanceMs | pitch (World) | pitch (snapshot) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| toggle tick | stand -> prone | 566.7 | 70.0 | 70.0 |
| +1 | stand -> prone | 533.3 | 45.0 | 45.0 |

From the second tick on the clamp holds ([-30, +45]); bots also stay in range (all-bot matches: 88 prone samples, 0
out of range).

## Suggested fix
Clamp the pitch after the stance request is resolved for the tick (or clamp with `pitchLimits` of the post-request
movement state). Keep the client prediction in step (`ClientGame` eases the camera into the same limits).

## Evidence
- `tests/sim/m2b-edge.test.ts`: "BUG-012 (fails until fixed) ..." and the control test "QA 9 control: one tick after
  a prone toggle at +70 deg the pitch is +45 ...".
