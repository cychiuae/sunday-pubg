# BUG-013: Island rifle kill share is above the 30 % ceiling after M2b (32.3 % / 33.9 %)

- **Severity:** minor (tuning target, combat.md AT22 says "tuning target, not a unit test"; the 35 % cap per weapon still
  holds, match length is in range).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18 (after the M2b C-gate). M2a measured rifle 29.6 % (idle) / 25.4 % (looter).
- **Suspected owner:** game-designer (`docs/design/balance.json`); the change since M2a is likely the carrier drop
  (bots land on loot clusters, `bots.drop.*`) and/or the bot ADS / nav changes (game-developer) rather than rifle numbers.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `npx tsx src/shared/sim/tools/matchReport.ts --matches 30 --variant both --map island01` (29.4 s wall).

## Expected
combat.md AT22 (and "Why the rifle no longer dominates"): median match 330-480 s; no weapon above 35 % of weapon
kills; the rifle between 15 % and 30 %.

## Actual
| Variant | Median match | Top weapon shares | Rifle |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle human | 371 s (274-440) | rifle 32.3 %, dmr 21.8 %, smg 20.8 % | **32.3 %** |
| looter human | 362 s (282-443) | rifle 33.9 %, smg 23.9 %, dmr 13.8 % | **33.9 %** |

The office is inside every target (rifle 23.4 % / 23.1 %, top weapon smg 29.1 % / 32.4 %, median 387 / 388 s).

## Suggested fix
Re-run with the M2b island loot and bot drop numbers and pick a lever (rifle loot weight, rifle falloff, or bot
weapon preference). Note that the looter human also died to the rifle most often (9 of 30).

## Evidence
`matchReport.ts` output in `docs/qa/M2b-test-report.md` "Pacing".
