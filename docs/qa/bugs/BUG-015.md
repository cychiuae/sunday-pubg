# BUG-015: Office loot group `ep.perchSW` has a single candidate (layout.md AC 3), and AC 2's per-group wording does not match the generator

- **Severity:** minor (fairness/variety: the SW perch epic is in the same place every match; no crash, totals correct).
- **Build:** working tree 2026-11-18 (M2c complete, balance v7).
- **Suspected owner:** level-designer (`src/game/levels/variety/office.variety.json`, `docs/design/levels/variety.md`
  §5.5); game-designer for the layout.md acceptance wording.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `npx vitest run tests/sim/m2c-layout-qa.test.ts` (the `it.fails` "BUG-015 (fails until fixed) ...").
2. Or read `office.variety.json` `lootGroups.epic`: `{"id": "ep.perchSW", "pick": 1, "candidates": [[-64.72, 19.815, "perch"]],
   "note": "p2 only: p1 would sit 29.7 m from spawn 16 (rule: 30 m)"}`.

## Expected
layout.md acceptance 3: "Every group has >= ceil(use x 1.5) and >= use + 1 candidates (data test, no seed)." For pick 1
that is 2 candidates.

## Actual
- `ep.perchSW`: 1 candidate; over 202 seeds it always activates (-64.72, 19.815). Every other group passes the rule
  measured from the outside (distinct activated points over 202 seeds >= the required count).
- Related wording issue (not a code fault): layout.md AC 2 says "every group activates exactly its `use` count". The
  generator keeps the per-tier totals exact on every seed (island 51 / 19 / 7, office 50 / 20 / 8, checked), but per group
  the count varies by design: the island beach ring alternates 1 / 2 per gap (variety.md §4.3 ringGaps phase) and office
  common groups `cc.ncorr` 3/2, `cc.aisle` 3/4, `cc.scorr` 3/2, `cc.rooms` 8/9/10 change when §5.4b tops up a spawn
  that is short of loot.

## Suggested fix
Add a second SW perch candidate that keeps the 30 m spawn rule (e.g. the NE perch has p1 and p2), or record the
exception in layout.md AC 3. Reword AC 2 to "per tier exact; per group exact except ringGaps phase and §5.4b top-ups".

## Evidence
`tests/sim/m2c-layout-qa.test.ts` output: `ep.perchSW: 1 distinct points over 202 seeds, need 2 (use 1)`;
`[layout office] tiers per seed {"common":[50],"rare":[20],"epic":[8]}`.

## Status: closed (fixed), 2026-11-18, M2c closing round
Verified: `ep.perchSW` has 2 candidates (office.variety.json). `tests/sim/m2c-layout-qa.test.ts` AC 3 now covers every
group with no exception and passes over 202 seeds per map. layout.md AC 2 was rewritten (tier totals exact; per-group
rules stated); the QA test checks the new rules: island POI / inland groups exact, the beach ring 36 commons in 12 gaps of
2 and 12 of 1, alternating; office epic / rare / guaranteed groups exact, plain commons summing to 34 on every seed. All
6 tests pass.
